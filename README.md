# Villarceau-Circle.Engine
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villarceau_circles Atkinson cycle, diesel or HCCI skyactiv X

it is a positive displacement piston compressor and expander made of two Tooidal Staters and housings with rotating Villarceau circle Pistons. it's a split phase engine where the compressor side always stays cold and the expander side always stays hot it has a separate combustion chamber with Henry Ricardo's swirl chamber characteristics at the center connects the two Toroids. and the intake and exhaust valves consist of a rotating disk at the center radial of the toroid passing through the inner wall where the diameter is smallest with a diagonal slice passing through that allows the piston to pass through it.

This is inspired by the Wankle rotary engine and "Masive Yet Tiny" engine (a Kevin Davie's Favorite) and many other rotary designs and theoretical heat engines! 

it runs most effectively in a braits and cycle similar to the star rotor engine, that has a gear inside of a larger gear with mushroom teeth similar to what's used in oil pumps. but this engine has piston rings and Sealing against the wall of the viller so circle shaped passage.

power can be taken off the engine from the central disc that also doubles as the rotary valve. Or the entire valve rotating assembly can be embedded with permanent magnets were even electromagnets in the outside housing can have a large surface area dedicated to electric coils. Making a very interesting range extender for hybrid or battery electric vehicles.

the break thermal efficiency is mainly determined by the diameter and volume difference between the two toroids: the intake compression versus expansion and Power. and it can be adjusted to run at different optimal RPMs using a 2nd electronic "gate" chamber valve between the combustion chamber and intake similar to how Toyota is Prius Atkinson or Miller cycle technology works with variable intake durations. if the second valve is in place? some of the compression stroke can be diverted to an accumulator that stores high pressure intake air for later use so it has properties of a supercharger as well but without a separate compressor machinery.
